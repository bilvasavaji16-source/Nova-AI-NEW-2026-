Information may be processed to operate accounts, provide AI responses, maintain security, and troubleshoot problems.

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect account information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices

6. Contact

For privacy questions, use the official Nevuxa support address provided on the website.
